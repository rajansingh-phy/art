# **UNESCO World Heritage**

The UNESCO World Heritage List consists of landmarks recognized for their cultural, historical, or scientific significance, showcasing humanity’s relentless admiration, determination, and understanding of the world.

## **Motivation**

Awestruck and motivated by the sheer determination of humans to create and preserve such marvelous structures that have endured the test of time.

## **Setup**

To learn and improve my skills following guidelines are abided:

1. Only 1 site is selected from the every country listed on the [UNESCO World Heritage Convention](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/)
1. The countries are grouped according to the beginning letter 
1. The constraints, medium and the style is same for all the countries belonging to the same group
1. No two groups can have same set of constraints

> **Note:** Click on the letter to view the group.
